Our Story

Ross' Rotisserie was founded in 2020 by Ross Jackson, a passionate chef with a dream of bringing authentic, flavorful rotisserie chicken to the community. What started as a small family operation has grown into a beloved local favorite.

Our commitment to quality has never wavered. We use only the freshest ingredients and time-tested cooking methods to ensure every chicken that leaves our kitchen is absolutely perfect.

Meet the Team

Ross Jackson - Owner & Head Chef

With over 20 years of culinary experience, Ross brings his expertise and passion to every dish. His secret spice blend is what makes our rotisserie chicken truly special.

Maria Rodriguez - Kitchen Manager

Maria ensures that every chicken is cooked to perfection. Her attention to detail and dedication to quality control makes her an invaluable member of our team.

Tom Chen - Customer Service Lead

Tom's friendly smile and exceptional customer service keep our guests coming back. He's always ready to help you find the perfect meal.
